Company Profile

A multi-disciplined Malawian contractor

Mkaka Construction Company Limited was established in 1993 and incorporated in 2000. Since then it has built the capacity to take on work of any size, or several works at once.

We are registered in the Unlimited Category for both Civil Engineering and Building Works with the National Construction Industry Council (NCIC). Clients include government institutions, the donor community and the private sector.

Our corporate culture focuses on integrity, teamwork, respect for all people and cultures, safety, corporate citizenship, innovation and total customer satisfaction.
