About Us

We build more than structures. We build trust.

Mkaka Construction Company Limited is a multi-disciplined Local Malawian Construction Company established in 1993 and incorporated in 2000.

We are registered in both Civil and Building in Unlimited Categories. Our philosophy is to exceed the expectations of our customers through integrity, teamwork, safety, innovation and total customer satisfaction.
