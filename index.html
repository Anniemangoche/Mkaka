About Mkaka

A multi-disciplined Malawian contractor.

Mkaka Construction Company Limited was established in 1993 and incorporated in 2000. We are registered in the Unlimited Category for both Civil Engineering and Building Works.

Our philosophy is to exceed the expectations of our customers through integrity, teamwork, safety, innovation and total customer satisfaction.

We hold our own plant and equipment, which lets us mobilise at short notice and take on work of any size, or several works at once.
